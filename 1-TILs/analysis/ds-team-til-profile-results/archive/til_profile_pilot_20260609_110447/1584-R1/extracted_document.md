BACKGROUND DISCUSSION
The turbine shell is a part of the general stator tube structure, and supports nozzles and shrouds. It contains high-
pressure combustion gases and compressor discharge air. The forward end of turbine shell is equipped with
compressor discharge air extraction ports for the inlet bleed heat (IBH) system. These ports are located on both sides of
the upper and lower half turbine shell, as shown in Figure 1.
Figure 1:
IBH cover flange locations on turbine shell
The first cracking incident was reported in 2005 after four years of operation. The crack initiated at the pipe to cover
flange weld as shown in Figure 2, and resulted in substantial hot air leakage.
Figure 2:
Photo of Pipe crack location on cover flange
This type of leakage can cause elevated turbine compartment temperatures that could lead to a forced outage
condition, and are also a safety concern.
This TIL is being issued to alert all 7FA customers of the potential for cracks to develop at this weld, and to provide GE's
recommendations regarding the frequency of inspections and corrective actions to be performed.
RECOMMENDATIONS
NOTE: All personnel must remain outside the gas turbine compartment while the gas turbine is fired and running, unless
absolutely necessary, in accordance with the GE Operation and Maintenance manual.
1. GE recommends dye penetrant inspection of the weld joint by a certified level II NDT inspector at the following
intervals:
a. First inspection at the first Hot Gas Path inspection (HGPI). If beyond first HGPI, then at the next planned
shutdown opportunity.

b. Re-inspection at every HGPI/Major inspection, or at a convenient planned outage.
2. If inspections indicate cracks on the pipe or on the weld, corrective action is recommended per the following
options:
Option 1: Repair
Grind off the cracked section of the structure. Use GE weld specification P8A-AG3 or equivalent to perform
repair welding.
Option 2: Replacement
Replace the flange with a new part. A new flange gasket will also be required. Install per the MLI-0705 assembly
drawing.
3. Re-inspect repaired, replaced, and original IBH weld joint per schedule in item 1 above.
