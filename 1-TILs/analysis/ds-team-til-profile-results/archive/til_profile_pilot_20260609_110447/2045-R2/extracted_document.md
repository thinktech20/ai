BACKGROUND DISCUSSION
In 2016 at a GE Vernova Service Center, a set of 7F Standard AGP S3Bs exhibited significant tip shroud deflections following
the first tour of service. Upon further destructive evaluation, a component exhibited metallurgical symptoms of creep in the
suction side and pressure side fillet regions of the tip shroud. The internal creep voids that were observed may propagate to the
surface and initiate cracks that could eventually lead to tip shroud liberation. There have been no forced outages or field
findings related to this issue in AGP S3Bs.
GE Vernova current AGP S3B repair procedure includes steps to mitigate the impact of creep over the component life-cycle.
However, additional modifications to the S3S and S3B are required to improve the affected S3Bs' resistance to creep distress.
As previously noted in the R0 release of this TIL, a S3S modification has been developed that supplies additional cooling air to
the S3B tip shroud to improve its creep life. This modification will be applied to affected S3Ss coming through GE Vernova
Service Centers and should also be applied to spare sets of affected S3Ss.
As also noted in the previous R1 release of this TIL, a new S3B repair process was in development. This repair is now available
through GE Vernova Service Centers and is intended to help improve the remaining creep life of affected standard AGP and
Tech S3Bs that have operated for their first tour with S3Ss that do not have the cooling air modification applied. As part of this
repair process, the amount of creep accumulated in each individual S3B will be evaluated to determine eligibility for this repair.
S3Bs that have operated for longer hours at higher temperatures and loads will tend to see more creep distress, and there is
the potential for some S3Bs to be scrapped due to accumulated creep.
RECOMMENDATIONS
Stage 3 Shroud Recommendations
The following actions should be completed for the affected S3S parts numbers listed in Table 1:
1. All affected spare sets of S3Ss should be sent to a GE Vernova Service Center to receive the cooling air modification
prior to installation and operation in a unit.
2. All affected S3Ss currently operating in a unit should be sent to a GE Vernova Service Center to receive the cooling air
modification after completion of current tour.
The above recommendations apply regardless of which AGP configuration (e.g., Standard, Tech, Tech-Lite, etc.) is installed in a
unit or that unit's hours/starts duty cycle. Affected S3Ss that receive the modification through repair will have the modification
number from Table 1 marked above the original machining part number on the front face of the shroud.
Table 1: Summary of Affected and Modified S3S Part Numbers

cooling air:
1. Resizing of ninth-stage extraction air orifice plate
2. Unit control constant update
3. Installation of updated inter-segment seals, i.e., laminate seals, on the following static components:

Stage 3 Bucket Recommendations
This TIL applies to the following S3Bs that have operated with affected (uncooled) S3Ss per Table 1.
Table 2: Summary of Affected S3B Part Numbers
These S3Bs may have accumulated creep distress and should be sent to a GE Vernova Service Center for evaluation and to
receive the new repair process. Upon receiving the new repair process, they will be marked with machining part number
129T6911.
Parts Tour After Repair
1. 7F.04 Standard AGP parts repaired with the new process should only be operated with modified (cooled) S3Ss in their
subsequent tour.
2. For 7F.04 Tech parts, eligibility for subsequent tour operation will be assessed during the repair process. If such parts
are determined eligible for subsequent tour, they must be operated with the modified (cooled) S3S configuration.
3. Upon receiving the new repair process, parts tagged with the following part numbers will be limited to ONLY one tour
after repair:
129T6911P0001 through P048
129T6911P0101 through P0108
After the completion of one tour - post-repair, the aforementioned parts must be scrapped.
In addition, there are a limited number of units operating with S3Bs that were previously refurbished before the new repair
process was implemented. As recommended in the previous R0 release of this TIL, these S3Bs should be Fluorescent
Penetrant inspected (FPI) every 4000 fired hours until removed from service. The FPI should cover both the pressure and
suction sides of the tip shroud fillets.
Please contact your local GE Vernova Service Representative for the new S3B repair process.
