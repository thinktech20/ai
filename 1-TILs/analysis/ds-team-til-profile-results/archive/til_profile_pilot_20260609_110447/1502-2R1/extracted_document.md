TECHNICAL INFORMATION LETTER
7F AND 9F AFT END COMPRESSOR RUBS
APPLICATION
All 7F and 9F Heavy Duty Gas Turbines
PURPOSE
To inform users to monitor equipment operation and condition, and to identify the onset, occurrence, and effects of aft
end compressor rubs (when the tip of the rotor blade rubs the casing). Rubs are evident when blade tip material is
adversely affected. Information in this TIL is supplemental to information in the Inspection and Maintenance Manual
provided with the gas turbine.
REASON FOR REVISION
To update the technical content of the TIL, including frequency of tip loss and recommendations when discovered.

If rubs or tip loss do occur, blending to increase
clearances can be performed at Major Inspections (MI)
or when the Compressor Discharge Casing (CDC) is
removed. During repairs, clearances of rotor blades can
be increased radially by tip grinding and polishing, and
tip corners repaired by blade blending. Typically, 20
mils radially of material can be removed to both
increase clearance and remove heat affected and
rolled over material.
Following this operation, a penetrant inspection must
be performed to ensure no tip cracks are present. If
the CDC is removed prior to the major inspection,
clearances can also be increased to eliminate the
potential for tip rubs should the customer desire.
