BACKGROUND DISCUSSION
GE 7F.05 compressors have a 14 stage compressor starting with rotor stage 1 (R1) and ending with rotor stage 14
(R14). Directly upstream of the R1 blades are the inlet guide vanes (IGVs). In addition, the first three stages of stator
vanes on a 7F.05 are variable stator vanes (VSVs). T-Fairings are removable hardware that form the rotor flowpath in
the forward compressor between rotor stages. For 7F.05 compressors, there are two stages of T-Fairings. The 1-2 T-
Fairing is between the stage 1 and stage 2 compressor blades, and the 2-3 T-Fairing is between the stage 2 and
stage 3 compressor blades. The location of VSV2 and 2-3 T-Fairing can be seen in Figure 1.

RECOMMENDATIONS
The following maintenance program is recommended to reduce the risk of VSV2 tip loss. During planned borescope
inspections, it is recommended to:
1. Inspect 100% of the VSV2s for evidence of tip loss or tip indications.
2. Inspect 100% of the 2-3 T-Fairings for rubs. Document the conditions of each T-Fairing so that rubs can be
monitored over time.
Please contact your GE representative with the inspection results, for evaluation and disposition by GE engineering.
The above borescope inspections are recurring until the below actions are completed.
During planned major inspections or planned outages when the compressor is exposed, it is recommended to:
1. Inspect the condition of VSV2 tips and document any rubs or tip curl with photographs. Following this, it is
recommended to tip the VSV2s to increase the radial clearance. Please contact your GE representative for
further guidance on this tipping.
The purpose of the VSV2 clearance increase is to reduce the likelihood of VSV2 tip rubs. This clearance
increase is expected to have a negligible impact on compressor flow, efficiency, and stall margin.
2. Inspect the 2-3 T-Fairing flow path surface and edges for evidence of high metal, burrs, or rubs. If high
metal, burrs, or rubs are noted, please document the condition of the T-Fairing's with pictures. Contact your
GE representative with the inspection results, for evaluation and disposition by GE engineering.
This TIL can be deemed complete if the vanes are tipped to increase the clearance or replaced with the VSV2s that
have an increased tip clearance.

Manpower Skills
For Bls: A qualified and competent GE borescope inspector.
For MIs: A qualified and competent GE blade technician. A competent and qualified non-destructive testing NDT)
inspector for post tipping NDT. For this work, NDT is considered a penetrant inspection. Flourescent penetrant
inspection (FPI) is recommended. Red dye penetrant, if FPI is not available, is acceptable NDT as well.
Scope of Work
During planned Bls,
Inspect 100% of the VSV2s for signs of tip loss or tip indications
Inspect 100% of the 23 T-Fairings for signs of severe rubs. Document the conditions of each T-Fairing so
that rubs can be monitored over time.
Please contact your GE representative with the inspection results, for evaluation and disposition by GE engineering.
During planned major inspections or planned outages when the compressor is exposed:
Inspect the condition of VSV2 tips and document any rubs or tip curl with photographs. Following this, it is
recommended to tip the VSV2s to increase the radial clearance.
Inspect the 2-3 T-Fairing flow path surface and edges for signs of high metal, burrs, or rubs. If high metal,
burrs, or rubs are noted, please document the condition of the T-Fairing's with pictures.
The annual inspections of VSV2 and 2-3 TF can be performed during the recommended inspection in TIL 2212.
Therefore, if the inspections in TIL 2212 are performed then annual inspections recommended per TIL 2167 are
complete.
Please note that the corrective actions (tipping or replacing the VSV2s) in this TIL are independent of the corrective
actions recommended in TIL 2212.
