BACKGROUND DISCUSSION
Wear due to stator rocking has been found in the S15 casing slot on a number of F-class units. The damage in the hook
fit for these cases was discovered during a visual inspection of the stators. In most cases, the square base of the stator
had worn to the point where the base was protruding into the airflow path (see Figure 1).

RECOMMENDATIONS
Inspection of the aft stator slots on all affected F-class units at the first available opportunity is recommended. During
planned combustion inspections (CI) and hot gas path inspections (HGPI), a thorough borescope inspection (BI) of the aft
stages (S14-S16) area should be performed. During major inspections or outages that require the removal of the
compressor discharge case (CDC), a stator rocking check and visual inspection of the stators and casing slots should be
performed.
If any significant component wear, stator rocking, or cracks are discovered, the damage should be documented with
pictures and submitted to your GE Energy Services representative for engineering disposition.
The following sections outline the aft stator rocking inspection with and without CDC removal.
Inspection Procedure 1 - Borescope Inspection during CI, HGPI, or yearly monitoring when aft stators are NOT
exposed:
Borescope inspection is recommended for units during planned CI, HGPI, or yearly monitoring if the CDC is not to be
removed. (If the CDC is to be removed please see Procedure 2.) During the borescope inspection look for the following in
Stages 14, 15 and 16 of the compressor:
Any indication that the base is protruding into the airflow path. Indications may appear more frequently on the
unloaded side of the stators. The unloaded side when looking aft in the lower half would be the left hand side,
and in the upper half it would be the right hand side. Figure 4 shows an example of the typical borescope
inspection measurement.
A large circumferential gap at the horizontal joint or any protruding or missing shims may be an indication of
stator rocking.
Any other evidence of cracks and/or excessive wear (fretting) between the square base platform of the stator
and the case or the next stator platform.
