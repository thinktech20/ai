TECHNICAL INFORMATION LETTER
HA AND .05 COMPRESSOR STATOR VANE RING DISTRESS
APPLICATION
Applicable to all 7F.04-200, 7F.05, 9F.05-18, 7HA.01, 7HA.02, 7HA.03, 9HA.01, and 9HA.02 units.
PURPOSE
The purpose of this TIL is to inform affected users about the potential for Vane Ring distress and the inspections needed
to monitor the said distress at the next scheduled outages. Failure to comply with this requirement may result in domestic
object damage to compressor airfoils.
REASON FOR REVISION
Fleet findings showed wear mainly on unloaded segments. This TIL revision is issued to inform the users of the updated
fleet findings and of the availability of a new vane kit that contains fully coated vane and ring unloaded segments.

BACKGROUND DISCUSSION
Affected units have a 14-stage compressor starting with rotor stage #1 and ending with rotor stage #14. Downstream
from rotor stage 14 is the stator stage 14 (S14) and the Exhaust Guide Vane (EGV) row.
Non-variable vanes are assembled into ring segments and the assembled segments are in turn inserted into the
compressor casing slots. Reference Figure 1.
Figure 1: Vane and ring segment assembly
Affected vane ring stages are those that interface with the Compressor Discharge Case (CDC). Affected stages are listed
in Table 1. Variable vanes and forward vane ring stages that interface with the Mid Compressor Casing are not affected.
Table 1: Affected Vane Ring Stages
Some of the affected units have reported distress at (1) the forward face of the stator vane ring segment and its
corresponding casing face, (2) the ring forward tab outer diameter (OD) face, (3) the ring aft tab inner diameter (ID) face,
and (4) the stator vane tip typically on the Vane trailing edge (TE), which interface with the rotor wheel, see Figure 2.

During machine normal operation, there could be relative motion between the ring segment and casing slot, and this
motion may be contributing to wear and distress at some interfaces with the Compressor Discharge Casing.
Updated fleet finding showed wear mainly on the S11 to S13 unloaded segment, while a few units had wear on the other
stages of CDC unloaded segments.
GE Vernova has developed a new vane kit, consisting of four "unloaded segments" (first two segments each on upper
half right horizontal joint and lower half left horizontal joint). This vane kit is available for each stage in the CDC. All the
segments in these kits are "fully coated" part, i.e., a coating is applied on stator ring and vane dovetail forward and aft
faces and stator ring forward and aft tabs to help mitigate the risk of distress on the stator ring faces and tabs. There is
no change in the stator vane and ring with the previous TIL revision, only this vane kit is now available.
RECOMMENDATIONS
The following maintenance program is recommended to monitor and address the risk of domestic object damage due to
distress seen on vane and ring segments.
1. During planned annual borescope inspections, it is recommended to:
Vane and Ring. platform - forward side: Inspect 100% of the affected stage Vane and ring platforms, focusing
on the ring forward side interface with casing for signs of material cracking and chipping.
Vane Tips: Inspect Vane tips on the first two segments from the upper half right horizontal joint and the first two
segments from the lower half left horizontal joint (forward looking aft), focusing on pressure side trailing edge
squealer tip region for signs of rubs; if rub is noted, continue inspecting the adjacent segments. Document the
conditions of any distress so that conditions can be monitored over time. See Figure 8 for example of rub on Vane
tips seen through borescope inspection.
Depending on the amount of distress seen, replacement of the vane and ring segment may be needed. The borescope
inspections recommended above can be utilized to monitor the condition over time and to plan for vane and ring segment
replacement.
2. During planned major inspections:
It is recommended to replace unloaded segments of S11 to S13 during a Major Inspection. Lead time for these
parts is generally high (in some cases longer than 1 year), therefore plan ordering replacement part well in
advance of your planned MI.
In addition to above unloaded segments replacement it is recommend to inspect segments that are not being
replaced per item 3 below.
3. During any planned/unplanned outages when the compressor stages under CDC are exposed, it is recommended
to inspect the areas highlighted in Figure 2:
Vane and Ring. platform - forward side: Inspect 100% of the affected stage vane and ring platforms for signs of
material cracking and chipping. All cracked/chipped vane and ring segments shall be pulled out from the casing
and inspected.
Vane Tips: Inspect 100% of affected stage vane tips for signs of rubs, focusing on pressure side trailing edge
squealer tip region. All worn TE tips segments shall be pulled out from casing and inspected.
Ring tabs: Inspect stator ring tabs for signs of wear, start with unloaded segments (See Figure 3); if wear is
observed on these segments, continue inspection on adjacent ring segments until there are no findings. If the
unloaded segments were already replaced with "fully coated" (with coating on Vane and Ring segment forward
