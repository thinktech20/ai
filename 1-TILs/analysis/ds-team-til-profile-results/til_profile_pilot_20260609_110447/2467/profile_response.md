## Purpose
To inform users of the need to inspect the compressor circumferential blades platform gap and plan MI accordingly.

## Reason For Revision
Not provided

## Compliance And Triggering Context
- Compliance Category Code: M
- Compliance Category Text: Maintenance - Identifies maintenance guidelines or best practices for reliable equipment operation.
- Recurring Indicator: Recurring - annual BI inspections and at each outage opportunity
- Coarse Outage Type: Next Scheduled Outage
- Maintenance Trigger Text: Annual borescope inspections, planned outage planning BI with at least 12 months lead time, any non-planned upper half casing opening, and Major Inspection outages.
- Completion Criteria: Circumferential blade gap measurements recorded and provided to GE representative for technical disposition; if gap exceeds 0.500 inches [12.7 mm], GE disposition obtained; post blade change out gap measurement documented if blade replacement performed.

## Scope Of Work
- Perform circumferential gap measurements as noted during annual BIs (approx. 2 additional hours added to standard BI).
- Perform circumferential gap measurements as noted during outage planning BI (approx. 6 additional hours added to standard BI).
- Perform gap measurement at casing open outage (approx. 4 additional hours during standard MI activities).
- If needed, perform circumferential blade swap out (blade balance charting may be needed as part of this scope); depending upon scope, may take 1 to 5 shifts.

## Service Recommendation Line Items
- At all annual BIs, for circumferential blade stages 4, 10, 13, and 14: perform visual inspection of circumferential blade lockers and measure circumferential blade gap in each sector.
- If gap is greater than 0.500 inches [12.7 mm] for any sector, contact GE representative for technical disposition.
- Maintain a record of circumferential blade gap measurements.
- At annual BI closest to planned outages (HGP or MI) with at least 12 months lead time, for all circumferential blade stages (4 through 14): perform visual inspection of lockers and measure circumferential blade gap in each sector for all stages.
- Provide circumferential blade gaps and trend records to GE representative for outage blade planning technical disposition to enable blade part procurement in advance.
- At any non-planned outage upper half compressor casing opening, for stages exposed by casing removal: perform visual inspection of lockers, measure gaps in each sector, maintain records, and provide measurements to GE for technical disposition.
- At Major Inspection outage with all compressor casing upper halves removed, for all circumferential blade stages (4 through 14): measure circumferential blade gaps in as-found condition prior to any stage disassembly.
- If any blade change out is performed, perform post blade change out circumferential gap measurement and document.
- Removal and replacement of compressor rotor blades shall be done to maintain rotor balance requirements; GE qualified blade technician recommended for any blade replacement activities.
- GE LES BI specialist to perform BI blade measurements; GE certified service technician/field engineer to perform casing open blade measurements.

## Recommended Interval Or Trigger
- Annual borescope inspections - stages 4, 10, 13, and 14
- Annual BI closest to planned outage (HGP or MI) with at least 12 months lead time - all stages 4 through 14
- Any non-planned outage upper half compressor casing opening - stages exposed
- Major Inspection outage with all compressor casing upper halves removed - all stages 4 through 14
- Timing Code 6 - Next Scheduled Outage
