## Purpose
To inform users to monitor equipment operation and condition, and to identify the onset, occurrence, and effects of aft end compressor rubs (when the tip of the rotor blade rubs the casing). Rubs are evident when blade tip material is adversely affected. Information in this TIL is supplemental to information in the Inspection and Maintenance Manual provided with the gas turbine.

## Reason For Revision
To update the technical content of the TIL, including frequency of tip loss and recommendations when discovered.

## Scope Of Work
- Monitor equipment operation and condition for aft end compressor rubs
- Integrate inspection for aft end compressor rubs with yearly scheduled borescope inspection per GER 3620
- Observe rotor blade stages 13 through 17 and look for mushroomed material, bluing at the blade tip, and/or markings in the casing area
- If rubs or tip loss occur, blending to increase clearances can be performed at Major Inspections (MI) or when the Compressor Discharge Casing (CDC) is removed
- During repairs, clearances of rotor blades can be increased radially by tip grinding and polishing, and tip corners repaired by blade blending (typically 20 mils radially)
- Following tip grinding/polishing, a penetrant inspection must be performed to ensure no tip cracks are present

## Service Recommendation Line Items
- Follow operating practices per GER 3620 to reduce the potential for rubs on aft end compressor blades
- Keep enclosure doors closed during startup, shutdown and normal operation
- Be aware that restarting the gas turbine between 2 and 8 hours following a shutdown or trip will cause clearances in the aft end to decrease; include this area in yearly borescope inspections if unit operates this way
- Eliminate any potential pipe leaks that may cause quenching of the gas turbine compressor discharge casing
- Ensure vent fans and exhaust fans to the gas turbine enclosure are functioning properly such that no local forced convection is taking place at or near the compressor discharge casing
- Integrate the inspection for aft end compressor rubs with yearly scheduled borescope inspection per GER 3620; observe rotor blade stages 13 through 17
- If rubs or tip loss occur, perform blending at Major Inspection or when CDC is removed; typically remove 20 mils radially to increase clearance and remove heat affected and rolled over material
- Following tip grinding/polishing operation, perform penetrant inspection to ensure no tip cracks are present

## Recommended Interval Or Trigger
- Yearly borescope inspection per GER 3620
- Next Scheduled Outage (Timing Code 6)
- Major Inspection (MI) or when Compressor Discharge Casing (CDC) is removed for blending repairs
