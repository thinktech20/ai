## Purpose
Communicate inspection recommendations to help mitigate the risk of possible damage to the gas turbine associated with turbine 1-2 spacer wheel rim cracking and possible rim section liberation.

## Reason For Revision
Not provided

## Compliance And Triggering Context
- Compliance Category Code: A
- Compliance Category Text: Alert - Failure to comply with the TIL could result in equipment damage or facility damage. Compliance is mandated within a specific operating time.
- Recurring Indicator: Non-recurring; considered complete once the inspection is completed and dispositioned. Further inspections completed as part of rotor life extension processes.
- Coarse Outage Type: Hot Gas Path or Major Inspection
- Maintenance Trigger Text: Turbine 1-2 spacer wheel has accumulated 80,000 factored fired hours of operation.
- Completion Criteria: This TIL is considered complete once the inspection mentioned in the recommendations section is completed and dispositioned. Further inspections will be completed as a part of any rotor life extension processes.

## Scope Of Work
- Upon removal of the turbine shell casing for the hot gas path or major outage, perform a thorough cleaning of the rim of the 1-2 turbine spacer wheel to remove any oil and/or residual debris from the outer surface (approximately one shift, ~8 hours).
- Perform ultrasonic inspection of the turbine 1-2 spacer wheel rim region (approximately two shifts, ~12-16 hours).

## Service Recommendation Line Items
- Perform ultrasonic inspection of the rim region of the turbine 1-2 spacer wheel at the first outage (either hot gas path or major inspection) once the turbine 1-2 spacer wheel has accumulated 80,000 factored fired hours of operation (approximate range for inspection is 80,000 to 96,000 factored fired hours).
- Engage GE's Inspection & Life Extension Services (I&LES) group to accurately perform the recommended inspection of the turbine 1-2 spacer wheel rim regions.
- Inspect both forward and aft rim arms of the turbine 1-2 spacer wheel.
- If indications are observed during inspection, contact GE service representative for assistance with disposition of the findings.

## Recommended Interval Or Trigger
