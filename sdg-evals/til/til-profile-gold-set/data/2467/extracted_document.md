BACKGROUND DISCUSSION
For 7F.04-200, 7F.05, 9F.05-18, and all HA class advanced compressors, the rotating blades for all stages are field
replaceable. Stages 4 through 14 are circumferentially loaded compressor blades into the compressor wheel dovetail slots.
Each circumferential blade stage has four lockers that separate the blades into typically four sectors identified as left, right, top
dead center (TDC), and bottom dead center (BDC). Each of these sectors has a required gap. Some field units have observed
increasing circumferential gaps at the blade platform in the various sectors as operation has occurred. To date, the observed
measured gaps have not specifically affected the normal operation of the gas turbine. However, GE is recommending
monitoring of these circumferential gaps in order to provide detailed planning actions as necessary to help ensure long term
operational capability of the unit.

RECOMMENDATIONS
Circumferential blade gap measurements are recommended to be taken as noted during various specific opportunities to aid in
circumferential gap monitoring as well as planning for parts for planned outage gap maintenance as required.
GE has identified step-by-step process to measure platform gap for all engine types/configurations. GE recommends that the
noted Borescope Inspections (BI) should be performed by GE's Life Extension Services (LES) group, and casing open
measurements should be recorded by GE field technicians/field engineer. Working closely with Product Service Engineering,
LES, and GE field technicians using developed inspection techniques and data recording methods will yield more accurate
inspection results and allow for analysis and disposition as rapidly as possible. While inspections performed by third parties
may yield some qualitative information, such inspections may not contain sufficient quantitative accuracy to allow for thorough
analysis and disposition.
Generally at all annual Bls, it is recommended to perform the following inspection steps:
For circumferential blade stages 4, 10, 13, and 14:
Perform a visual inspection of the circumferential blade lockers.
Perform visual inspection of blade gaps in each sector - measure circumferential blade gap in each sector for stages.
If gap is greater than 0.500 inches [12.7 mm] for any of the sectors, please contact your GE representative for technical
disposition.
Maintain a record of circumferential blade gap measurements.
At annual BI inspection closest to planned outages (i.e., Hot Gas Path outage or Major Inspection outage) with at least one
year (12 months) of lead time, it is recommended to perform the following inspection steps:
For all circumferential blade stages (4 through 14):
Perform a visual inspection of the circumferential blade lockers.
Perform visual inspection of blade gaps in each sector - measure circumferential blade gap in each sector for all stages.
Provide circumferential blade gaps and any trend records noted from general annual BI inspections to your GE representative
for outage blade planning technical disposition (enable blade part procurement as needed in advance of outage timeframe).
At any special case (non-planned outage) upper half compressor casing opening situations, compressor case for forward
section stages, or compressor discharge case for aft section stages, it is recommended to perform the following inspection
steps:
For circumferential blade stages exposed by casing removal:
Perform a visual inspection of the circumferential blade lockers.
Perform visual inspection of blade gaps in each sector - measure circumferential blade gap in each sector for all stages
exposed.
Maintain a record of circumferential blade gap measurements.
Provide circumferential blade gap measurements and any trend records noted from general annual BI inspections to your GE
representative for a technical disposition.
Measurements taken in this manner can cover those specific stage values recorded to satisfy BI measurements for that year
per the two BI recommendations above.

At Major Inspection outage with all compressor casing upper halves removed (compressor case and compressor discharge
case), it is recommended to perform the following inspection steps:
For all circumferential blade stages:
Perform a visual inspection of the circumferential blade lockers.
Perform visual inspection of blade gaps in each sector and measure the circumferential blade gaps for all stages (4 through
14) in the as-found condition prior to any stage disassembly efforts.
Provide circumferential blade gap measurements and any trend records noted from general annual BI inspections to your GE
representative for a technical disposition.
If any blade change out is performed on any particular stages, then GE recommends a post blade change out circumferential
gap measurement be performed and documented.
Maintain a record of circumferential blade gap measurements.
Removal and replacement of compressor rotor blades shall be done in an appropriate manner to maintain rotor balance
requirements and subsequent vibration capabilities. It is recommended that a GE qualified blade technician perform any blade
replacement activities

Scope of Work
Perform circumferential gap measurements as noted during annual Bls (approx. 2 additional hours added to standard
BI).
Perform circumferential gap measurements as noted during outage planning BI (approx. 6 additional hours added to
standard BI).
Perform gap measurement at casing open outage (approx. 4 additional hours during standard MI activities).
If needed, perform circumferential blade swap out (blade balance charting may be needed as part of this scope);
depending upon scope, may take 1 to 5 shifts.
