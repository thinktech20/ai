Provide snippets only for substantive engineering fields where the document text is the primary evidence — for example: `scope_of_work`, `service_recommendation_line_items`, `failure_consequences`, `risk_summary`, `recommended_interval_or_trigger`, `parts_referenced`, `severity_signals`.
